University Event – Do’s and Don’ts

All students are hereby informed to strictly follow the guidelines mentioned below to ensure that Jhoom 2.0 is conducted in a safe, disciplined, and dignified manner.

DO’s

Mandatory Guidelines
  • Students must report at the designated time and venue as instructed by the University.
  • Students must follow the event theme and dress code and come in proper attire as prescribed for Jhoom 2.0.
  • Students must maintain proper discipline, decorum, and respectful behaviour throughout the event.
  • Students must strictly follow the instructions of the Faculty Coordinators, Event Committee, and University authorities.
  • Students are expected to maintain proper grooming and professional conduct.
  • Students should cooperate with fellow students, faculty members, security personnel, and event coordinators.
  • Students must take care of University property and the event venue.

DON’Ts

Strictly Prohibited
  • Misconduct, indiscipline, abusive language, misbehaviour, fighting, harassment, or any inappropriate activity will not be tolerated.
  • Students must not engage in any activity that damages the reputation, dignity, or discipline of the University.
  • Students must not disturb or disrupt the event, classes, performances, or other University activities.
  • Students must not damage University property, decorations, equipment, or venue facilities.
  • Students must not enter restricted areas or leave the designated venue without permission.
  • Any form of misbehaviour, inappropriate dress, obscene gestures, or unacceptable conduct will invite strict disciplinary action.
  • Students must strictly adhere to the prescribed theme-based attire.

Terms & Conditions

Any student found involved in misconduct, indiscipline, damage to property, harassment, or any other serious violation of University rules shall be liable to:

Financial Penalty A fine of ₹2,000/-
and
Academic Penalty Suspension from attending classes for the entire semester Subject to the University's disciplinary rules and due process.

The University reserves the right to take additional disciplinary action in serious cases as per the applicable University regulations.

All students are advised to maintain the dignity and reputation of the University and make Jhoom 2.0 a safe, respectful, and memorable celebration.

By Order of the University Authorities
